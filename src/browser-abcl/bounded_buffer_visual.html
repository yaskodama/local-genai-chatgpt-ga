JavaScript-runtime version. One Producer actor and one Consumer actor share a capacity-20 Buffer actor. Adjust each side's speed with the sliders below — make the producer faster than the consumer and the buffer fills up and the producer blocks; flip it and the consumer blocks on the empty buffer. The numbers on each side show how many more operations can complete without blocking.
