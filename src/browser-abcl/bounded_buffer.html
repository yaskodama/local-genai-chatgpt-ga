ABCL/c+ — Bounded Buffer (producer / consumer, capacity = 20)

One Producer actor and one Consumer actor share a capacity-20 Buffer actor. The producer runs every ~80 ms and the consumer every ~250 ms, so the buffer fills, the producer blocks, and each consumer-get wakes the queued producer. Blocking is expressed in ABCL's native way: the buffer stashes the waiting sender and hand-delivers a reply when the guard becomes satisfiable.
