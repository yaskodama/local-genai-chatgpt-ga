ABCL/c+ — Bounded Buffer (producer / consumer, capacity = 4)

One Producer actor and one Consumer actor share a capacity-4 Buffer actor. The producer runs every ~120 ms and the consumer every ~420 ms, so the buffer fills, the producer blocks, and each consumer-get wakes the queued producer. Blocking is expressed in ABCL's native way: the buffer stashes the waiting sender and hand-delivers a reply when the guard becomes satisfiable.
