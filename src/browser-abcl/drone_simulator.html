ABCL/c+ — Drone Return-Route Support Simulator

Multi-agent port of Taga et al. (2016) — the smartphones of stranded commuters are replaced with a swarm of relief drones. Each Drone actor scans for impassable zones, diffuses discoveries through a MANET-style broadcast, and detours around known obstacles while flying toward the safe zone.
